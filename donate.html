Partnering with Jubilee Jobs

We accept donation of gently used or used with minor repair. We do not accept children's bikes or eBikes. We reserve the right to refuse a bike that isn't fixable or too costly to fix.

The donation will be made to Jubilee Jobs of Lexington.

Jubilee Jobs of Lexington works to offer hope to all in Central Kentucky who desire to work but face barriers to securing stable, dignified employment.
